# E-Commerce Financial & Retention Performance Analysis (2012–2015)

## 1. Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on evaluating the financial performance, customer retention, marketing channel efficiency, and product profitability of an e-commerce toy store.

The analysis covers a three-year period (March 19, 2012 – March 19, 2015) and aims to identify business growth trends, revenue drivers, customer purchasing patterns, and the impact of refunds on profitability.

## 2. Dataset

- **Source:** Maven Analytics – Toy Store / E-Commerce Dataset
- **Time Period:** March 19, 2012 – March 19, 2015
- **Structure:** 6 relational tables

The dataset includes:

- Website Sessions
- Orders
- Order Items
- Order Item Refunds
- Products
- Website Pageviews

Key data points include customer activity, order details, product sales, marketing channels, revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), and refunds.

## 4. Project Workflow

### Step 1: Data Importing & Cleaning – Excel & VBA

- Imported and inspected all 6 relational datasets.
- Standardized date-time formats and column headers.
- Identified and handled missing attribution values.
- Validated primary and foreign key relationships.
- Used VBA macros to automate data inspection and standardization.

### Step 2: Data Analysis – PostgreSQL & SQL

- Imported cleaned datasets into PostgreSQL.
- Wrote SQL queries using joins, CTEs, aggregate functions, and window functions.
- Calculated financial KPIs and period-over-period growth.
- Analyzed marketing channel performance and conversion rates.
- Segmented new and returning customers.
- Evaluated product profitability and refund impact.
- Generated 5 aggregated CSV files for Tableau visualization.

### Step 3: Dashboard Development – Tableau

- Connected SQL analysis outputs to Tableau.
- Created KPI cards and interactive visualizations.
- Added year and month filters.

- Designed an executive dashboard to communicate key business insights.

## 6. Key Findings & Business Insights

**1. Overall Growth & Profitability**

- Net revenue reached $1.85M, representing 417.9% growth.
- Net gross profit reached $1.13M, with 422% growth.
- Order volume increased by 394%, while AOV grew by 23.53%.

**2. Marketing Channel Performance**

- gsearch generated the highest net revenue (\~$1.2M).
- Unassigned and bsearch achieved higher conversion efficiency despite lower traffic.
- socialbook lagged in both revenue and conversion rate.

**3. Customer Acquisition & Retention**

- New customers generated nearly all monthly revenue.
- Returning customers contributed a negligible share of sales.
- Customer retention emerged as a major growth opportunity.

**4. Product Profitability**

- The Original Mr. Fuzzy led in gross profit (over $700K) and average sales velocity (\~22 units/day).
- The Hudson River Mini Bear ranked second in daily sales velocity (\~12.3 units/day), despite being the latest launch.

**5. Seasonality & Refund Impact**

- Revenue peaked between November and February (\~$200K+ monthly).
- Sales declined during April–August.
- Refunds consistently reduced gross revenue, with no significant seasonal spikes.

## 7. Business Recommendations

- Introduce product bundles and cross-selling strategies to increase AOV.
- Reallocate underperforming socialbook ad spend toward high-converting bsearch campaigns.
- Improve customer retention through loyalty discounts and post-purchase email campaigns.
- Increase inventory and marketing visibility for high-velocity products.
- Plan inventory and marketing budgets ahead of peak sales months.
- Introduce seasonal promotions to improve mid-year sales.
